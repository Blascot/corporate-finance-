## Executive Summary

I am selecting PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk (IDX: TLKM / NYSE: TLK) for the remainder of this project. Telkom is Indonesia's largest state-owned telecommunications and digital infrastructure conglomerate (Rp146.7tn revenue, FY2025), currently executing one of ASEAN's most significant corporate restructuring sequences: the July 2023 IndiHome fixed-mobile convergence (FMC) merger into Telkomsel, and the December 2025 partial spin-off of its Wholesale Fiber Connectivity assets into PT Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia (TIF), now branded InfraNexia. These back-to-back events have produced clear, auditable ratio deterioration across five years — EBITDA margin declining from 53.6% (FY2022) to 49.2% (FY2025), ROA from 12.2% to 8.5%, and interest coverage from 19.6x to 13.9x — making TLKM a rich, data-dense subject for structured ratio analysis. Five years of audited consolidated financials (FY2021–FY2025) are available from the company's 2025 Annual Report (610 pp., filed May 2026) and SEC Form 20-F filings on EDGAR.

---

## Background

Telkom is Indonesia's incumbent telco with the Government of Indonesia holding a 52.09% majority stake. Operations span four segments: mobile (Telkomsel, 156.1mn subscribers), data and IT services (enterprise B2B and IndiHome broadband), wholesale and international (Telin, 14-country footprint), and tower infrastructure (listed subsidiary Mitratel, IDX: MTEL). Reporting currency is Indonesian Rupiah (IDR); accounting standard is PSAK, converged with IFRS. Fiscal year ends December 31.

The analytical interest centers on three structural events in the FY2023–FY2025 window: (1) the IndiHome FMC merger into Telkomsel (July 2023), which required restatement of FY2023 and FY2024 comparables per PSAK 208 / Note 2.z.iii; (2) the InfraNexia spin-off executed December 2025, shifting consolidated asset and revenue composition; and (3) an Early Retirement Program (ERP) incurring Rp1,186bn in FY2024 and Rp937bn in FY2025, directly compressing operating margins during the restructuring window.

## Method

Primary source is the PT Telkom Indonesia 2025 Annual Report (audited, 610 pp., filed May 2026), which provides a five-year financial highlights panel (FY2021–FY2025) covering income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement, capex, and key ratios. This will be supplemented by SEC Form 20-F (FY2024, filed April 28, 2025; FY2025 filing expected mid-2026) for IFRS note disclosures including lease schedules (PSAK 73 / IFRS 16) and segment-level EBITDA. IDR/USD exchange rates will be sourced from Bank Indonesia (period-end, December 31 each year). Market capitalization and share price history will be drawn from the Indonesia Stock Exchange (idx.co.id). ASEAN telco peer metrics (Axiata, Globe Telecom, True Corporation) will be sourced from Bloomberg for relative benchmarking.

All YoY comparisons will use the restated FY2023 and FY2024 figures from the 2025 Annual Report. Telkom's own leverage ratio definitions include finance lease liabilities in both numerator and denominator (per PSAK 73); this treatment will be disclosed explicitly in ratio model assumptions.

---

## Findings

The five-year audited panel reveals a sustained, multi-dimensional ratio decline coinciding with Telkom's restructuring cycle:

**Profitability** has compressed materially and continuously. EBITDA margin fell from 53.6% (FY2022) to 49.2% (FY2025) — the first sub-50% reading in the panel — driven by flat revenue (Rp147.3tn in FY2022 vs. Rp146.7tn in FY2025) against rising depreciation and amortization (up 10.1% YoY in FY2025 to Rp37,649bn) from prior capex cycles. Net income attributable to the parent fell 28.1% from Rp24,760bn (FY2021) to Rp17,814bn (FY2025), reducing net income margin from 17.3% to 12.1%.

**Coverage and leverage** have deteriorated. EBITDA-to-interest-expense declined from 19.6x (FY2022) to 13.9x (FY2025) as EBITDA fell 8.5% while finance costs rose from Rp4,652bn (FY2023) to Rp5,206bn (FY2025). Debt-to-EBITDA rose from 0.8x to 1.0x over the same window — still conservative in absolute terms, but directionally consistent with increased debt drawn to fund dividends and capex during the integration period.

**Asset efficiency** has declined. ROA fell from 12.2% (FY2021) to 8.5% (FY2025) — a 370 basis-point compression — as total assets grew modestly (+3.8%) while net income fell sharply, a classic pattern during high-investment restructuring cycles.

**Liquidity and cash generation** remain healthy. Operating cash flow grew 3.6% YoY to Rp63,842bn in FY2025, and the current ratio improved slightly to 83.5% — providing a partial counterpoint to the profitability and coverage headwinds.

---

## Implications

The ratio trends establish three analytical hypotheses for the full project:

1. **I expect EBITDA margin to remain below 50% in FY2026** because the InfraNexia spin-off (December 2025) transfers high-margin infrastructure assets out of consolidated operations, and the FY2025 D&A run-rate (Rp37,649bn, +10.1% YoY) has not yet stabilized post-capex cycle.

2. **I expect interest coverage to decline further toward 12–13x by FY2026** because finance costs are sticky (Telkom's bond covenants are fixed-rate, Rupiah-denominated) while EBITDA is under revenue and margin pressure with no near-term catalyst for recovery.

3. **I expect ROA to trough in FY2025–FY2026 and begin recovering in FY2027** because the ERP workforce reduction (FY2024–FY2025) and capex optimization (Rp24.4–24.6tn in FY2024–FY2025, down from Rp32.9–34.2tn in FY2022–FY2023) should translate to cost relief in the medium term once restructuring charges normalize.

The most relevant ratio categories for TLKM are profitability (trend analysis across the restructuring window), leverage/coverage (debt sustainability), and efficiency (capex-to-revenue, asset turnover). Liquidity ratios serve as a secondary health check.

---

## Limitations & Next Steps

The InfraNexia spin-off was executed in December 2025, so its full balance-sheet impact will first appear in FY2026 comparables — the current panel captures only TIF's first partial year as a consolidated subsidiary (Rp4,455bn revenue, Rp612bn EBITDA). FY2023 and FY2024 figures are restated (Note 2.z.iii); any prior-year sources (pre-restatement info memos) cannot be used for YoY comparisons without adjustment.

Next steps: extract the full five-year ratio panel from the Annual Report into the Stage 1 template, compute peer benchmarks against Axiata and Globe Telecom, and flag any PSAK-to-IFRS differences material to leverage ratio interpretation.
