## Executive Summary

I am selecting PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk (IDX: TLKM / NYSE: TLK) for the remainder of this project. Telkom is Indonesia's largest state-owned telecommunications and digital infrastructure conglomerate, currently executing two back-to-back structural events: the July 2023 IndiHome fixed-mobile convergence (FMC) merger into Telkomsel, and the December 2025 spin-off of wholesale fiber assets into PT Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia (InfraNexia). These events have produced auditable, multi-dimensional ratio deterioration across five fiscal years, making TLKM a data-dense subject for structured ratio analysis. Five years of audited consolidated financials (FY2021–FY2025) are available from the 2025 Annual Report and SEC Form 20-F on EDGAR.

---

## Background

Telkom is Indonesia's incumbent telco; the Government of Indonesia holds a 52.09% majority stake. Market cap: approximately IDR 265 trillion (~USD 16.4bn; IDX: TLKM, December 31, 2025). Industry: telecommunications and digital infrastructure. Operations span four segments: mobile (Telkomsel, 156.1mn subscribers), data and IT services (enterprise B2B and IndiHome broadband), wholesale and international (Telin, 14-country footprint), and tower infrastructure (listed subsidiary Mitratel, IDX: MTEL). Reporting currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Accounting standard: PSAK, converged with IFRS. Fiscal year ends December 31. The FY2023–FY2025 window captures three overlapping restructuring events — the IndiHome merger, the InfraNexia spin-off, and an Early Retirement Program (ERP, Rp1,186bn in FY2024 / Rp937bn in FY2025) — that together produced the ratio deterioration motivating this analysis.

---

## Method

Primary source is the PT Telkom Indonesia 2025 Annual Report (audited, 610 pp., filed May 2026), providing a five-year financial highlights panel (FY2021–FY2025) covering income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, and capex. Supplementary sources: SEC Form 20-F (FY2024, filed April 28, 2025) for IFRS note disclosures and lease schedules (PSAK 73 / IFRS 16); Bank Indonesia for period-end IDR/USD rates; IDX for share price and market data; Bloomberg for ASEAN peer metrics (Axiata, Globe Telecom, True Corporation). All YoY comparisons use restated FY2023–FY2024 figures per Note 2.z.iii of the 2025 Annual Report.

---

## Findings

Three falsifiable hypotheses guide the ratio analysis:

1. **I expect EBITDA margin to remain below 50% in FY2026** because the InfraNexia spin-off (December 2025) transfers high-margin infrastructure assets out of consolidated operations, and the FY2025 D&A run-rate (Rp37,649bn, +10.1% YoY) has not stabilized post-capex cycle. Margin has already fallen from 53.6% (FY2022) to 49.2% (FY2025).

2. **I expect interest coverage to decline toward 12–13x by FY2026** because finance costs are sticky (fixed-rate, IDR-denominated bonds) while EBITDA faces simultaneous revenue pressure and margin compression, with no near-term recovery catalyst. Coverage fell from 19.6x (FY2022) to 13.9x (FY2025).

3. **I expect ROA to trough in FY2025–FY2026 and begin recovering in FY2027** because ERP workforce reductions and capex optimization (Rp24.4–24.6tn in FY2024–FY2025, down from Rp32.9–34.2tn in FY2022–FY2023) should translate to cost relief once restructuring charges normalize.

---

## Implications

The most relevant ratio categories for TLKM are **profitability** (EBITDA margin trend across the restructuring window), **leverage and coverage** (debt sustainability given rising finance costs), and **efficiency** (capex-to-revenue and asset turnover during the spin-off period). Liquidity serves as a secondary health check. Financial-sector structures — net interest margin, capital adequacy ratios — are not applicable; TLKM is a non-financial industrial conglomerate.

---

## Limitations & Next Steps

The InfraNexia spin-off was executed December 2025, so its full balance-sheet impact first appears in FY2026 comparables — the current panel captures only TIF's partial year (Rp4,455bn revenue, Rp612bn EBITDA). FY2023–FY2024 figures are restated (Note 2.z.iii); pre-restatement sources cannot be used for YoY comparisons without adjustment. Next steps: extract the full five-year ratio panel into the Stage 1 template, compute peer benchmarks against Axiata and Globe Telecom, and flag PSAK-to-IFRS differences material to leverage ratio interpretation.
